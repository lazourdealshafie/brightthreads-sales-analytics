# BrightThreads | Retail Sales & Growth Analytics

**Meta Data Analytics Course Capstone | OSEMN Framework**

## Project Overview

BrightThreads is a fictional New York clothing boutique operating both online and in-store. This course capstone examines whether the business could realistically achieve a **10% increase in annual sales**.

Using the OSEMN framework (Obtain, Scrub, Explore, Model, iNterpret), I evaluated data quality, examined sales and marketing patterns, explored predictive modeling, and translated analytical findings into business recommendations.

## Business Objective

Evaluate BrightThreads' 10% sales-growth goal and identify opportunities to improve revenue through inventory planning, marketing efficiency, and customer insights.

**Primary KPI:** Total sales revenue, measured monthly and year over year.

## Analytical Approach

### 1. Obtain & Scrub

Identified relevant data sources, including sales transactions, customer information, advertising expenditure, and website traffic.

Evaluated data-quality issues such as duplicate transactions, inconsistent formatting, missing fields, and incorrect pricing values.

### 2. Explore

Analyzed Q1 online sales data comprising 58 order-level records.

Examined order values, revenue concentration across product categories, and potential opportunities for feature engineering.

### 3. Model

Examined the relationship between social media advertising expenditure and website traffic using linear regression.

Interpreted customer segments and evaluated seasonality as a consideration for sales forecasting.

### 4. iNterpret

Translated analytical results into recommendations focused on inventory allocation, advertising efficiency, and business performance monitoring.

## Key Findings

| Analytical Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Order values | Median order value of $79.99 |
| Sales distribution | Shoes generated the highest category revenue |
| Advertising and traffic | Strong positive correlation (r ≈ 0.99) |
| Traffic projection | Approximately 12 site visits per week at $250 weekly advertising spend |
| Customer segmentation | Higher-value repeat shoppers and occasional budget-conscious shoppers |
| Sales forecasting | Quarterly patterns suggested the value of seasonal forecasting methods |

The advertising relationship is correlational, and forecasts beyond the observed spending range should be interpreted cautiously.

## Business Recommendations

**Inventory optimization:** Prioritize consistently high-performing product categories while evaluating demand for lower-performing items.

**Marketing efficiency:** Reallocate existing advertising spending toward stronger-performing channels before increasing the overall budget.

**Performance monitoring:** Review revenue, site visits, and marketing engagement over the following six months to evaluate progress.
